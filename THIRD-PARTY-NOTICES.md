- **Author:** ozan.pl (Ozan Tezcan)
- **License:** MIT
- **Used for:** the file-count module (per-folder element counts in the file

- **Upstream:** https://github.com/cimuyang/folder-pin-view
- **Author:** cimuyang
- **License:** MIT
- **Used for:** the file-pinning module (pinning notes and folders to the top of

- **Upstream:** https://github.com/iBlinkQ/immersive-folder
- **Author:** Blink (iBlinkQ)
- **License:** MIT
- **Used for:** the drag-to-reorder interaction (the drag handle, the

- **Upstream:** https://github.com/Akifyss/obsidian-border
